About the numbers

Ventilation rate = mixing height (ft AGL) × transport wind (kt), in knot-feet, rated on the New Mexico smoke-management scale: Poor <40k, Fair 40–60k, Good 60–100k, Very Good 100–150k, Excellent ≥150k. Transport wind is the mean wind through the mixed layer (10-m wind plus GFS pressure levels inside it). 10-m wind is at 33 ft; 20-ft winds run somewhat lower. Forecast: Open-Meteo (NOAA GFS + HRRR), ~0.75° grid, refreshed ~4×/day. Perimeters: NIFC WFIGS current perimeters. Boundaries: NIFC National GACC Boundaries. Coarse-grid model guidance — terrain-driven drainage and inversions in valleys will be worse than shown. Always get a spot forecast.
