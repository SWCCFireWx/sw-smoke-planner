About the numbers

All forecast fields come from the NOAA National Blend of Models (NBM) CONUS 2.5-km grid, averaged to a 0.1° (~10 km) grid and smoothly interpolated on the map. Mixing height, transport wind and 10-m wind/gusts are NBM's own fields. Ventilation rate = mixing height (ft AGL) × transport wind (kt), rated on the New Mexico smoke-management scale: Poor <40k, Fair 40–60k, Good 60–100k, Very Good 100–150k, Excellent ≥150k knot-feet. Chance of precip is NBM's probability of ≥0.01" in the 1-hour (3-hour after 48 h) period ending at the selected time. 10-m wind is at 33 ft; 20-ft winds run somewhat lower. Perimeters: NIFC WFIGS. Boundaries: NIFC GACCs. Valley inversions and drainage winds can be worse than any grid shows — always get a spot forecast.
